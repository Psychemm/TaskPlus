Your task lists, in color.

Task List Colors is a free browser extension for Google Calendar. It colors every Google Task by the list it belongs to, and lets you show or hide individual task lists from My calendars, just like calendars.

What it does

  • A color for every listTasks take their list's color in Day, Week, Month and Schedule views, and in the task details popup.
  • Show or hide listsEach task list gets its own checkbox under My calendars, right below Tasks.
  • Calendar's own palettePick from the same 24 colors Calendar uses, or any custom color.
  • Feels built inRows and menus match Google Calendar's design, in light and dark themes.

How it works

Google Calendar's page doesn't say which list a task belongs to. With your permission, Task List Colors reads your task lists from the official Google Tasks API using read-only access, then colors the tasks on your calendar to match.

Everything runs in your browser. There is no Task List Colors server: requests go directly from your browser to Google, and your task data stays on your device. Read the privacy policy for details.
